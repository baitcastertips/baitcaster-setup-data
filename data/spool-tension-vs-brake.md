# Spool tension vs brake: two different controls

These two controls get treated as one dial. They are not, and confusing them is a common
reason a reel nests no matter how the dial is set.

## Spool tension

A constant drag that applies at all speeds. It mostly affects the **start and end** of the
cast.

## Brake

Speed-sensitive on a centrifugal system, and roughly constant on a magnetic one. It mostly
affects the **fast middle** of the cast.

## Why the distinction matters

Because they act at different points in the cast, a problem at the start of the cast and a
problem in the middle of the cast are not the same fault, and turning up the wrong control
will not fix it.
